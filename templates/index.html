About QRCraft

QRCraft is a free QR Code Generator designed to make creating QR codes quick and accessible. You can create QR codes for websites, text, Wi-Fi, contacts, email, phone numbers, WhatsApp, locations and photos.

Privacy

QRCraft is designed to generate normal QR codes directly in your browser. The text used for normal QR generation is not intentionally stored by QRCraft.

Photo QR generation may require an image to be sent to the QRCraft service so that a usable URL can be created. Do not upload confidential or sensitive photographs.
