Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Normal QR generation

For normal link, text, Wi-Fi, contact, email, phone, WhatsApp and location QR codes, the QR data is generated in your browser. The site does not need to send that content to our server.

Photo QR

If you use Photo QR, the selected image is uploaded temporarily to create a unique photo URL. Images are kept in server memory for a limited period and are automatically removed after expiry or when the service restarts. Do not upload sensitive or confidential images.

Local storage

The website may store a small recent-history list in your own browser using localStorage. This data is not an account database and can be cleared by clearing site data in your browser.

Security

Uploads are size-limited, validated as images and re-encoded before being served. The application also uses common browser security headers. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid entering secrets you do not need to share.

Third parties

The QR library used by the web app is loaded from jsDelivr. Your browser may connect to that provider when the library is not already cached.

Changes

This notice may be updated when features or hosting arrangements change.
